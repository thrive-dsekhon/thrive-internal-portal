Key Policy Rules at a Glance

These rules come directly from the Core Care Agreements (effective November 11, 2026). For full terms, open the agreement for the client's coverage type.

✍️ How to cancel

Written notice only — text or email to the therapist's work number/email, email to admin@thrivespeech.net, or a message through the Client Portal.

  • Phone calls, voicemails, and in-person messages do not count.
  • On time: written message received ≥ 2 hours before the visit start. The timestamp Thrive receives is the official record.
  • Late cancel: less than 2 hours' written notice.
  • No-show: client misses the visit with no written notice at all.

💳 Late cancel / no-show fee

Commercial & Self-PayMedicaid & CHIP
  • Commercial & Self-Pay — $50 fee per occurrence, charged to the card on file. Not billed to insurance. Non-refundable even if a make-up is scheduled.
  • Medicaid & CHIP — No fee, but each late cancel or no-show still counts toward the attendance limits below.

🚫 When no fee applies (all clients)

  • Thrive cancels or closes.
  • Client develops a stay-home symptom less than 2 hours before the visit and written notice is sent before the start time.
  • An official severe-weather warning is in effect for the client's area and written notice is sent before the start time.
  • A medical emergency prevented notice and proof is sent within 7 days.

📅 Attendance — 75% per month

  • Each calendar month: attended ÷ scheduled visits must be ≥ 75%.
  • Visits cancelled by Thrive, clinic closures, weather absences, Thrive-placed holds, and approved medical holds are excluded. Make-ups within 14 days fill their missed slot.
  • If attendance falls below 75%, Thrive reaches out in writing to make a plan.
  • After 2 late cancels or no-shows within 30 days → recurring time may be released; open appointments offered instead.
  • After 3 within 60 days, or below 75% for 60 consecutive days → Thrive may end services. Written notice given before either step, with records and referrals.

🌡️ Illness — when to stay home

Mild cough or runny nose without fever: OK to attend (mask recommended). Keep the client home and notify the therapist if any of the following apply:

  • Fever ≥ 100.4°F → return after 24 hrs fever-free without fever medicine.
  • Vomiting or diarrhea in the past 24 hrs → return after 24 hrs symptom-free.
  • Strep throat or impetigo → return after 24 hrs of antibiotics and no fever.
  • Pink eye → return after 24 hrs of treatment with no active redness or discharge.
  • Hand, foot, and mouth disease → return after 24 hrs fever-free without fever medicine and once blisters have dried.
  • COVID-19, flu, and other respiratory illness → return after 24 hrs fever-free without fever medicine and when symptoms are improving. Mask recommended for the next 5 days.
  • Other contagious conditions → return when no longer contagious; doctor's note may be required.
  • Open or bleeding wounds → must be covered, healing, and not at risk of infection.

💳 Card on file & payment

Commercial & Self-Pay
  • A valid credit, debit, or HSA/FSA card must be on file before the first appointment and for as long as the client receives services.
  • Copays and deductible amounts are charged on the day of the visit.
  • Remaining balance after insurance processes: statement sent via Client Portal/email → charged 10 days later.
  • HSA/FSA cards cannot be used for the $50 late cancel fee — keep a regular card on file for that.
Medicaid & CHIP
  • No card required. Families are not billed for covered Medicaid services.
  • If Medicaid coverage ends, visits pause until restored — or the family may choose in writing to continue at the self-pay rate.
